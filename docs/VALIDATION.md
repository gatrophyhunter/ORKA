# How accurate is ORKA? (validation against published data)

These numbers are the honest answer to "is it better than GRT?". They are regenerated by `npm run data` (`scripts/calibrate-powders.ts`, then `scripts/validation-summary.ts`) and shown in the app's **Validation** tab. The numbers below come from the build of 2026-11-23.

## Method

- **Data:** published, pressure-tested loads:
  - Hodgdon 2025 Annual Manual: Hodgdon, IMR, Winchester, Accurate and Ramshot powders; piezo psi, and CUP for some cartridges.
  - Reload Swiss 2025 guide: pressures in bar.
  - Shooters World manual.
  - Vihtavuori 2026 guide: velocities.

  In total there are 34,138 loads, and 30,845 had enough sourced inputs to simulate. CUP pressures are never converted to psi, so those loads contribute velocity only.
- **Held-out test (leave-one-cartridge-out):** each powder is refitted *without* one cartridge's loads and then predicts that cartridge's published velocities and pressures.
  - Only cartridges whose test-case brass capacity is published (by the brass maker or a measured comparison) are held out.
  - Training for the test uses only loads with published capacities, so no inferred quantity leaks into it.
  - Up to 6 cartridges per powder are held out.

## Results (held out)

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Pressure comparisons | 359 (48 powders, rifle cartridges only) |
| Median absolute pressure error | **12.7 %** |
| 90th-percentile absolute pressure error | **31.4 %** |
| Mean signed pressure error (bias) | **−5.3 %**, i.e. the model predicts *lower* pressure on average, which is the unsafe direction |
| Predictions within ±10 % | 38 % |
| Velocity comparisons | 570 |
| Median / 90th-percentile velocity error | 71 fps / 230 fps |

In-sample (the fitted data itself), rifle loads reach a median of 8.9 % (90th percentile 24.5 %). Handgun loads are clearly worse at 17.1 % (39.9 %). No handgun cartridge had a published test-case capacity, so **handgun accuracy could not be validated on held-out data at all.**

## What this means

- **ORKA's pressure predictions are not accurate enough to develop loads from.** A typical prediction is off by about ±13 %, and one in ten is off by more than ±31 %. On average the model reads about 5 % low.
- GRT users report around 3 % error for well-developed powder models, per a forum post cited in `research/raw/competitors.md`. That claim could not be verified, but ORKA is clearly **not** at that level. The main reason is that GRT's powder models come from closed-vessel measurements, while ORKA's are fitted to published load data. Published data carry the publisher's test components (brass capacity, bullet bearing surface), and those are mostly unpublished.
- What ORKA **does** offer:
  - It states its error. Every prediction shows the measured model error, an upper estimate, and a sensitivity band sized from that powder's own fit.
  - It puts the maker's published loads for your components beside the prediction and flags any charge above the published maximum.
  - Every number traces to a source document.
  - Comparisons between powders in the parametric search are relative, so they are less affected by the shared biases than absolute pressures are.
- Calibrate with **your own chronograph data** (Calibrate tab) for your rifle and components. Velocity calibration narrows velocity error, but it cannot validate pressure.

## Known contributors to the error

1. **Test-case capacity.** Most publisher test cases (Winchester, Hornady, Remington brass) have no published capacity. For 16,194 of 30,845 loads, an "effective capacity" was inferred from the data itself.
2. **Bullet length.** Hornady and Sierra publish no bullet lengths, so 23,774 loads use an estimated length. The leave-one-out error of the estimator is 6.8 % for pointed bullets and 7.5 % for blunt bullets.
3. **Model structure.** There is one shot-start pressure per construction class (fitted: monolithic 47.3 MPa vs the 25 MPa jacketed reference), no bearing-surface or jump effects, a single form function per powder, and Lagrange gradients.
4. **Burn-rate charts.** Chart-interpolated "estimated" powders have an RMS burn-rate error of 40 % when re-estimated for calibrated powders. They are labelled "not for load development".

## Data that could not be used

- **Loads that could not be simulated** (out of 34,138):
  - 942 cartridges not in SAAMI/C.I.P. (wildcats)
  - 762 cartridges without bore/groove dimensions
  - 1,318 loads without COAL or barrel length
  - 498 loads whose powder isn't in the database
  - 1,022 loads with no capacity that could be inferred
  - 271 loads without a usable bullet length
- **Hosts that could not be reached:**
  - Western Powders (Accurate/Ramshot product pages): connection reset. Those powders come from Hodgdon's chart and manual instead.
  - ADI: unreachable.
  - Alpha Munitions: bot checkpoint, not circumvented.
  - Hornady's catalog CDN.
  - shop.hodgdon.com.
